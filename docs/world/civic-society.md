# Civic Society

Government is layered rather than a single system-wide office. Planetary, regional, municipal and special-purpose authorities divide roads, water, ports, schools, hospitals, zoning, courts and taxation differently from place to place.

Law is modeled through institutions, evidence and remedies. A regulation matters because an inspector, court, port authority, employer or insurer can act on information—not because the universe applies an invisible morality meter.

Culture is similarly plural. Species have histories and environmental traditions, but billions of people produce regional identities, class differences, subcultures, religions, media, sports and fusion cultures. Culture enters play through food, events, passenger traffic, architecture, schedules and ordinary conversation rather than encyclopedia bonuses.
