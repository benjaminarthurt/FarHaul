# Geographic and Street-Level Scale

Far Haul uses two complementary kinds of detail. **Authored anchors** provide memorable places; **deterministic generated geography** supplies the thousands of ordinary settlements and facilities required by populated planets.

A homeworld with billions of residents must not feel like six cities surrounding one spaceport. Region seeds generate ordinary cities, towns, farms, industrial parks, utility sites and transport nodes reproducibly. Important generated places can be promoted into persistent authored records when the player interacts with them.

Inside settlements the same rule continues through districts, neighborhoods, blocks and individual sites. The final delivery address can therefore be a machine shop or clinic rather than the name of an entire city.
