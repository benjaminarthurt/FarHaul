# Physical Cargo Lots

A commodity becomes freight only when it is packed into a physical lot. Lots have mass, dimensions, package count, environmental limits, seals, ownership and custody.

Eight containers of grain and two refrigerated containers of pharmaceuticals occupy different space, require different handling and fail differently. A damaged crate does not necessarily destroy an entire contract, and a broken seal is evidence rather than an abstract penalty.

Cargo can be split, consolidated and transferred between carriers where packaging and contracts permit. Oversized freight can force route and ship-layout decisions before departure.
