# Ordinary Businesses and Addresses

Most freight ends at an ordinary organization: a supermarket, machine shop, hospital supplier, hotel, contractor, warehouse or utility. These businesses are generated from local population, income, land use and industrial demand rather than scattered randomly.

Businesses have employees, suppliers, customers, inventory and failure risk. Interaction promotes generated businesses to persistent entities, allowing the same repair shop, broker or restaurant to recur later.

Addresses follow local built environments. An Ilos delivery may be a span/level/unit; an Orun address can be junction/branch/cell; a Vey or Pelagos destination can be a platform and berth. The destination therefore communicates something about the world before the player arrives.
