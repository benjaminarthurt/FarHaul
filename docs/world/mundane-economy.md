# Mundane Goods

Civilization moves far more than exotic minerals and ship modules. Construction consumes cement, glass, insulation, pipe, cable and fasteners. Hospitals continuously consume sterile goods and medical gases. Households buy packaged food, beverages, clothing, paper products, detergents, furniture and appliances. Maintenance departments live on bearings, filters, valves, pumps and lubricants.

Most of this trade is local or regional on mature worlds. Interstellar freight becomes important when a world specializes, lacks local capacity, experiences disruption, or is too small to manufacture economically. That prevents the absurd result where Earth imports every box of detergent from another star while still giving frontier haulers endless ordinary work.
