# Manufacturing and Bills of Materials

Manufactured goods are dependency graphs rather than magic outputs. Engines need machine components, controls and thermal hardware; habitation modules need structure and life support; life-support racks themselves require pumps, electronics and treatment equipment.

The first BOM set intentionally uses normalized industrial units. Its purpose is to establish propagation topology before final mass and throughput balancing. If pump production falls, thermal systems and life support can both tighten; that can delay ship construction and settlement projects even when hull plate remains abundant.
