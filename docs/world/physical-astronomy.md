# Physical Astronomy

Star systems are physical spaces. Stars have mass and luminosity; planets have mass, radius, gravity, orbital elements, rotation and atmosphere. Derived values should agree rather than being independent flavor numbers.

Orbital position affects local transfer geometry. Far Haul can compress long travel for playability without pretending that every moon and planet is permanently the same distance apart. Faster transfers should trade against energy, propellant, heat or equipment capability.

The astronomy model is also the basis for procedural frontier generation, so ordinary unexplored systems obey the same physical rules as authored inhabited systems.
