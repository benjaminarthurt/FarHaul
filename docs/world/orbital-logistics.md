# Orbital Logistics

Surface-to-orbit is part of freight, not a loading-screen abstraction. Gravity, atmosphere, regulation and local infrastructure determine whether cargo lands aboard the interstellar ship, rides a shuttle, or crosses an orbital warehouse.

Small operators gain flexibility from landing capability. Very large freighters gain payload efficiency by remaining in space but inherit transfer fees, schedules and dependency on tugs, shuttles and orbital storage. This creates a real economic distinction between a box truck and a container ship.
