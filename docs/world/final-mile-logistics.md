# Final-Mile Logistics

Interstellar freight is only one leg of a shipment. Cargo can pass through customs, orbital transfer, rail or marine linehaul, a distribution center and a local delivery vehicle before reaching its consignee.

Custody changes are recorded. This makes damaged cargo, missed connections and insurance claims attributable to evidence instead of assuming the player controlled every stage.

Local vehicle types follow the built environment: road haulers on conventional colonies, cargo boats on marine worlds, freight crawlers in subsurface networks and vertical cars on Ilos. Final-mile logistics therefore reinforces planetary identity while creating smaller jobs for players and NPC carriers.
