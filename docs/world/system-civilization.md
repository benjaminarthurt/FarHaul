# System-Scale Civilization

A star system is not a planet-selection menu. Commercial systems contain transfer stations, depots, relays, mining infrastructure, rescue anchorages and local corridors. Traffic density and information freshness decline outward.

The player can therefore haul within a system, stage cargo between surface and orbital carriers, encounter congestion, choose fuel stops, wait for transfers, or operate beyond routine rescue coverage. Named facilities are anchors; busy core systems also contain aggregate minor stations, satellites and service traffic.
