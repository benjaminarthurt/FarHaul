# Exploration at Scale

Beyond supported space are far more ordinary systems than authored destinations. The frontier generator defines 1,200 cataloged stellar targets, with most commercially uninteresting. Red dwarfs, barren rocks, ice, gas giants and mediocre asteroid belts dominate.

Surveying is useful even when nothing valuable is found. Navigation fixes, orbital catalogs, atmospheric data, landing-site certification and communications plans reduce uncertainty for later travelers. Geological and biological work can be valuable, but extraordinary discoveries remain rare.

Survey data has ownership and licensing rules. Finding a mineral signature is evidence and information; it is not automatic sovereign ownership of a planet.
