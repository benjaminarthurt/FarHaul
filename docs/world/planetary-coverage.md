# Planetary Coverage Expansion

Known inhabited worlds are modeled as layered places rather than single destinations. Each homeworld has multiple regional settlement patterns, while named metros remain anchors inside much larger aggregate populations.

Physical geography constrains infrastructure and trade. Keshar's shelf cities are shaped by weather and load-bearing engineering; Ilos distributes population vertically between basins and escarpments; Veyara's inhabited geography includes pelagic networks rather than treating ocean as empty space; Orun's regional geography is primarily a connected subsurface network. Concord is deliberately less species-specific because centuries of multispecies settlement shaped its built environment.

The structured regional profiles are mechanics-facing coverage. Future authored cities, towns, utilities and industries attach to these regions rather than bypassing them. Aggregate population continues to consume food, utilities, services and freight even when individual settlements are not named.
