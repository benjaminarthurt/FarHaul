# Population and Persistent People

Population statistics generate people rather than replace them. Age, household, employment, income, education, migration and commuting distributions create plausible residents for each settlement.

Species influences environmental needs and some social traditions but is deliberately not an occupation or personality class. A Kesh accountant, Vey machinist, Human aquaculture engineer and Orun hotel manager are ordinary possibilities.

Most residents can remain aggregate until interaction matters. Once someone becomes a contract contact, passenger, crew member, witness, creditor, regular customer or relationship, that person becomes persistent with a home, job, schedule, relationships and remembered history.
