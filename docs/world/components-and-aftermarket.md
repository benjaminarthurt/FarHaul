# Ship Components and Aftermarket

Ships use identifiable equipment models with production eras, compatibility families and parts ecosystems. A discontinued reactor can remain perfectly usable for decades while becoming progressively harder to support away from industrial centers.

OEM, aftermarket, reconditioned and salvaged parts coexist. Cheap parts are not automatically bad and branded parts are not magically safe; condition, provenance, compatibility and installation quality matter.

This gives old ships personality through logistics. Keeping a forty-year-old freighter working may depend less on raw money than knowing which yard still rebuilds its pumps and which frontier chandlers stock adapters.
