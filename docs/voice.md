# Far Haul's voice

Everything a player reads is written in one voice: the game, the info site, the release notes and the
player-facing parts of the docs. This is the default. New text starts here.

## Who's talking

A tired, sardonic freight captain who has done this run a thousand times and still likes the job, a
bit. Dry jokes, sarcasm, and a steady low grumble about the bank, the tugs, the paperwork, physics,
the ship and the developer. Never mean to the player: they're the new captain, and the voice is on
their side against the universe.

## Rules

- **Every joke still says what to do.** A player who skims must still get the key, the place, the
  price and the consequence. Funny second, clear first, never vague.
- **Aim the sarcasm at the game, the ship, the universe, the bank and the developer, never at the
  player.** "The bank would like its tug back" is fine. "You ran out of fuel again, genius" isn't.
- **One joke per message, at most.** Many messages need none. A warning that saves the player from
  losing the ship, the cargo or their money is plain first, with the joke (if any) after.
- **Real numbers stay real.** Keys, fees, distances, limits and names are exact. The joke goes around
  them, never instead of them.
- **Keep it short.** In the game, a message stays about the length of what it replaces: HUD lines and
  prompts have little room. On the site and in notes, a sentence or two per point.
- **Key-reference lines stay plain.** The "W/S throttle  Z cut ..." strips and the install steps are
  reference, not prose: no jokes there.
- **Errors and refusals say what's wrong and how to fix it,** in the voice, without apologising.
  "Not enough credits for the berth: 900 cr short. The bank remains unmoved."
- **Plain words.** No in-jokes the player can't get, no memes, no swearing, nothing that dates fast.
- **Sentence case** for buttons and headings, as before. CAPITALS only where the game already uses them
  to name a place or desk to walk to (FREIGHT OFFICE, BAR).

## Examples

| Plain | In the voice |
|---|---|
| Out of fuel. Press Esc to call a tug (900 cr). | Out of fuel. Esc calls a tug: 900 cr, payable in advance, sympathy extra. |
| You've arrived. Deliver the load at the freight office to be paid. | You made it. DELIVER THE LOAD at the freight office before anyone asks where it's been. |
| Point the nose within 5° of the target first. | Point within 5° of the target first. You're 12° off. The ship can steer, not read minds. |

## Where it doesn't apply

Developer docs (`docs/runtime/`, code comments, test output) stay plain and technical: they're read by
people fixing things, who have enough problems.
