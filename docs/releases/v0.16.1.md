Far Haul 0.16.1 for Windows, in which you are no longer the only scavenger in known space, and the others have paperwork.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.16.1.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they're already claimed. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Claim beacons
- Within 2 km of a wreck, the radio (Y) offers to **fire a claim beacon** at it. A green light goes on the wreck and its tag says YOUR CLAIM. Legally it's yours. Practically, see below.
- You start with two. The repair desk sells more, 150 cr each, four to a rack. So does the ship remote.

### Rival salvage crews
- Some wrecks draw a rival salvage tug. Sometimes it's already there with its own beacon on, and the wreck says CLAIMED. Sometimes it turns up while you're working, and calls in to tell you how it's going to be.
- They park alongside and cut at their own pace: the panels, then the cargo hatch, then the hold. You'll see the torch. What they take is gone.
- **Hail them** to:
  - **Split it.** They take every other piece that's left and leave the rest to you. The cargo hatch opens for both.
  - **Tell them to clear off.** That usually works if your beacon was on first, rarely if theirs was. Standing helps either way. You get one go.
- Or race them. Cutting is a sport now.
- Take pieces off a wreck they claimed, or off their half of a split, and they complain to the port authority. That costs you 2 standing when the flight ends. Strip a wreck clean and they lose interest. The claim goes with them.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, at a dignified pace.
- Rivals only work the outside of a wreck. Nobody else goes aboard. Yet.
- The voices are still babble. Recorded lines for the regular calls come later.
