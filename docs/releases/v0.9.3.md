Far Haul 0.9.3 for Windows, in which money starts growing on trees, but only if you ask nicely in Settings.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.9.3.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they're worth exactly nothing. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Debug cheats
- For testing ships and flight without hauling freight for a month first. Off unless you turn on **Debug cheats** in Settings.
- Then the pause menu (**Esc**) has a **CHEATS** section, in port, in the shipyard and in flight:
  - **+1,000,000 cr**. In the shipyard you can spend it straight away.
  - **Repair and resupply**: hull to 100%, every working part as new, patch and spares kits full. In flight it also fills the tanks and dumps the heat, and un-breaches a breached hull.
- The save remembers that you cheated, and the pause menu mentions it. Quietly. Every time.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- No god mode, no free parts, no teleporting. A million credits buys most of what you'd want to test.
