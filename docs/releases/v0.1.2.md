Far Haul 0.1.2 for Windows: fixes and clearer flying, from the first playtest of 0.1.1.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.1.2.zip` under Assets below (not the Source code files), unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Finding the way
- When the target is off screen, an orange arrow near the edge points to it, with its distance. On the approach it points to the station.
- The direction-of-travel markers are labelled TRAVEL and REVERSE. When travel points somewhere off screen, it gets its own yellow arrow.
- The readouts show how far your course is off the target, and how to correct it.

### The braking autopilot stops you at the target
- On a run, **X** turns the ship tail-first and coasts while there's room, counting down to the burn. It then burns to stop at the target, taking out sideways drift on the way.
- Started too late, it warns that you'll overshoot.
- Moving away from the target, or outside a run, it stops you where you are.
- **X** again or any control takes over.
- Sitting back down at the helm no longer cancels it.

### Time compression you can see
- The rate shows top right. **/** goes straight back to x1.
- Every press of **,** **.** or **/** shows on screen.
- When time is held at x1, the readout says why and when it will work: leaving the station (counting down to 3 km out), on the approach, near the target, or at the brake point.
- Time compression now keeps working while the autopilot brakes, until you're within 25 km of the target.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation.
- Prototype numbers: prices, pay and tuning will change.
- No space walks, planets with air or crew yet.
