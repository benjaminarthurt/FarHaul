Far Haul 0.10.0 for Windows, in which it turns out you were never alone out here. Some of the others are even alive.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.10.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and there's nobody in them. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Other ships
- The other haulers in the economy now show up. Ships hold station behind every port, named for the companies trading there, with nav lights blinking and a name tag when you're close.
- A ship or two comes and goes: one burns out from the station into the dark, another comes back in.
- On a long run, now and then someone passes the other way along the route, a kilometre or two off.
- They're solid. Fly into one and you bounce off, the hull pays, and their insurer will be in touch.

### Derelicts
- Most days there's a dead ship drifting a few kilometres from the station, tagged DERELICT with its distance, from anywhere.
- Fly over, stop alongside, close enough for the tether, and go outside.
- The orange outlines on its hull are salvage panels. Take the **torch** (**2**), hold **E**, and cut one free. A few seconds of sparks and it's in your hands.
- Haul it back to your hatch, one panel at a time, and it goes in the locker. Reeled in with it? It comes too. Drift off with it? It doesn't.
- Sell the salvage at any exchange. A cut panel stays cut; the wreck doesn't grow new ones, though another wreck usually turns up another day.
- The wreck's hull is solid. You can't drift through it, however much you'd like to.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Other ships don't talk to you, trade with you, or dock while you watch. They hold station, come and go, and pass.
- No boarding derelicts from the inside yet: salvage comes off the hull.
- Other ships are built from the same few layouts you can build. Variety is coming with the manufacturers.
