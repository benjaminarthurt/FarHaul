Far Haul 0.16.0 for Windows, in which everyone out there finally has a radio, and opinions to put on it.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.16.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they don't take calls. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### The radio
- Press **Y** anywhere aboard: at the helm, walking the ship, out on the tether, or standing on a moon in a suit. You get everyone in range, nearest first, and what you can say to each. Click, or press the number.
- Every call comes through a bad radio: a chime, a squelch, hiss that gets worse with distance, and a voice. The voices aren't words yet. They're each species' own babble: Kesh low and slow, Ilyan high and clipped, Vey quick, Orun slow and warm. The words type out at the bottom of the screen as they speak. The same ship always sounds the same.
- The ship remote has a Comms card too, for answering from the sofa.

### Clearance
- Call **traffic control** before you dock and they give you a berth, and the collar's lamps go green. Sometimes they tell you to hold while another ship comes out first. You'll see it go.
- **Dock without clearance** and the clamps still engage. So does the fine: 300 cr, a point of standing, and a lecture you can read at your leisure.
- Moon bases want a call before you land, and give you a pad. Mining camps want a call too, and point at the flat bit. The flatter bit.
- Letting traffic control bring you in (T) counts as clearance, and autopilot runs call in for you.
- Come in hot near the collar and traffic control will mention it. They don't fine you for that. Yet.

### Other ships
- **Buy fuel** from a hauler parked by the port. Dearer than the port, but they come alongside. Hold still while they pump, or they'll tell you to.
- **Ask for news.** It comes from the live economy: who's short of what, whose prices are moving, whether there's a wreck nearby. Cheap, and sometimes worth more than the fuel.
- **Out of fuel?** The port tug still comes, by relay. A ship in range may offer a tow too, for less or for more. Out on a run, they know you have no choice.

### Maydays
- Now and then on a run, a ship that ran dry or lost its drive calls for help, SOS beacon and all. It's tagged in red, and the braking autopilot (X) stops you alongside.
- **Sell them fuel** at a rescue rate, or **take the crew aboard**. They're survivors, with the rescue bounty at the next freight office, and some of them might sign on. No room? **Report them** and the port sends its own boat.
- Fly past without doing anything and word gets round.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, with a slight delay on the line.
- The voices are babble, not speech. Recorded lines for the regular calls are planned.
- Rival salvage crews, and claim beacons to settle who was first, come next in 0.16.1.
