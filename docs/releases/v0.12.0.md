Far Haul 0.12.0 for Windows, in which one captain's wreck becomes another captain's upgrade.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.12.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and nothing in them is second-hand. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Fitting salvaged parts
- Parts you've hauled off derelicts now show up first in the shipyard palette, under **FROM YOUR HOLD**, each with its condition and a fitting fee.
- Fitting one costs **15% of the part's price new** (250 cr at least) instead of the full price. An engine off a wreck goes on for 3,000 cr instead of 20,000.
- It comes as worn as you found it. A 62% engine is a 62% engine; the space walk wrench and the yard's overhaul work on it like any other.
- It comes out of the hold when it goes on, which frees up the space. Undo puts it all back, fee included.
- **Shift+click** a salvaged part to unbolt it: it goes back in the hold at whatever condition it's in now, and you get the fitting fee back. It needs room in the hold, and the hold doesn't negotiate.
- The ship's value counts what you paid, not what it'd cost new. The bank was very clear on that.
- Would rather have the money? The shipyard desk still buys them.

### The FTL drive wears now
- The jump drive is a working part like the engine: every jump wears it a little.
- A failed drive doesn't jump. The ship says so, and the interstellar boards close until it's fixed.
- That makes the drive you strip off a wreck stranded by a failed jump exactly as dead as it sounds. Fit it cheap, then bring it back with a spares kit on a space walk, or have a yard overhaul it. Cheaper than new. Considerably less shiny.

### Also
- Removing or adding parts in the shipyard no longer shuffles their wear onto the wrong parts. The engine you serviced stays serviced.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Clearing the ship or starting again from the starter ship in the shipyard throws away fitted salvage along with everything else. It's called Clear for a reason.
- Every engine is still the same engine. Manufacturers are on the list.
