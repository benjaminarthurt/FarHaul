Far Haul 0.9.6 for Windows, in which a door cap stops hogging the space outside the door.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.9.6.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load, caps and all.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Caps, properly
- A door cap is now just a plate on the doorway. It no longer takes up the grid cell beyond it.
- So you can bolt a radiator, a tank, an engine or anything else on the outside, right over a capped doorway. A capped doorway counts as bare hull for mounting.
- Shift-click on the cell takes off whatever's bolted there first, then the cap.
- One cap to a doorway. The yard won't sell you two for the same hole.

### Hatch caps
- **Hatch cap, shaft top** and **Hatch cap, shaft bottom** (250 cr each) close the deck hatch in a ladder shaft's top or underside. Place them from the cell above or below.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
