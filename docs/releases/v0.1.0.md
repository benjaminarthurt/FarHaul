First playable prototype of Far Haul for Windows.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.1.0.zip`, unzip it anywhere (a USB stick works) and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, so they travel with it.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

You can also play in the browser: https://benjaminarthurt.github.io/FarHaul/

### In this build
- Ship builder: design a ship from modules and check power, cooling, thrust and the hull.
- New game, five save slots, and a dock at your starting system.
- A living freight economy across eighteen systems, on Easy, Normal or Hard.
- Local runs flown by hand or by autopilot, with docking.
- An FTL drive upgrade and star jumps.
- Get up from the helm (G) and walk the ship in first person.
- Theme music and the new intro video.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation.
- Prototype numbers: prices, pay and tuning will change.
- No planets, space walks or crew yet.
