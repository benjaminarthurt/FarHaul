Far Haul 0.9.1 for Windows, in which the ship remote stops being a passenger and starts pulling its weight.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.9.1.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they can't fly anything. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

The ship remote is the page you open on a phone or tablet when **Ship remote** is on in Settings. It got busy.

### More of the helm
- **Braking thrusters**: hold the button and they fire, let go and they stop. Like B, but in your hand.
- **Manoeuvring thrusters**: a slide pad, up, down, left and right, held like Ctrl and the arrows.
- Both only show if the ship has them fitted. The remote doesn't sell thrusters; the shipyard does.
- **Engage the FTL drive** turns up when you're flying a jump, and tells you what's stopping it until you're clear, slow and cool enough.
- Lose signal with a thruster held and it lets go by itself, rather than braking you into next week.

### Space walks
- Out on the tether, the phone shows your **air** as a countdown, the **tether** paid out and the **strain** on it.
- **Reel me in** brings you back aboard. **Cut the engines** stops the ship from flinging you about.
- Snap the tether and it says so, along with how far from the hatch you've drifted. It can't jet you home. Nothing can, except the jets.

### The ship and its crew
- A new panel lists everyone aboard and what they're doing right now: the engineer's next service, the deck tech waiting for the engines to go quiet, the pilot with the helm while you're up.
- Every working part's condition, the patch and spares kits aboard, the hold and the load.

### In port
- The remote does the desks now: **Freight**, **Crew** and **Repairs**.
- Take a job off the board, depart on autopilot, deliver, wait a day.
- Hire from the bar and let people go, two taps for the second so nobody gets sacked by a pocket.
- Buy patch kits and spares kits, repair the hull, overhaul the parts.
- The game's own screen keeps up: an open desk redraws, and when the ship moves or a day passes the place rebuilds around you.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Flying a run yourself still starts in the game, at the gate. So does the shipyard.
- No steering from the remote. Pointing the ship stays at the helm, where you can see what you're pointing at.
