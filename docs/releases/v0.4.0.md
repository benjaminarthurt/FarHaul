Far Haul 0.4.0 for Windows, in which somebody finally hands the captain a map.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.4.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they contain exactly zero maps. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It's a freighter game. It now comes with directions.

### Press M
- **M** opens the map in flight, on foot aboard, in a suit, and in ports. M or Esc closes it. The ship keeps flying while you read, so don't read for too long.
- **Tab** switches between your system and known space. The mouse wheel or **+/-** zooms, and zooming out far enough steps you out to known space anyway. Drag to pan, **Home** to recentre.

### The system chart
- The star, the orbits, the belts and every planet and moon the system has, by name. Sol's are where they really are, give or take some squashing so Saturn fits on the screen.
- Your port, fuel depot, moon base, belt works and (in Sol) the outer stations are marked. The crowd round the main world gets a close-up in the corner, so the labels stop sitting on each other.
- On a run, the route is drawn from where you left to where you're going, with the ship along it by how far you've actually got. The panel says how far to go, how fast you're closing, and when you'll arrive, in game time and in real time at your current time compression. Useful when deciding whether there's time to make a sandwich.
- Two arrows on the ship: blue for where you're actually going, green for where the nose points. When they disagree, physics is about to have a word.

### Known space
- All eighteen systems from above, joined by their trade routes (busier routes, thicker lines), coloured from the core out to the frontier.
- Your system is ringed in green. On a jump, the destination is ringed in amber with a line to it, so you know which direction to be nervous in.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- The system chart is a chart, not a scale model: orbits are squashed so the inner worlds and the giants fit together. Sizes are flattering.
- Prototype numbers: prices, pay and tuning will change, usually at the worst possible moment.
- No space walks, planets with air or crew yet. You now know exactly how far away they are.
