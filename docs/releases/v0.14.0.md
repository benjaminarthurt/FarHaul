Far Haul 0.14.0 for Windows, in which it turns out somebody else makes engines too, and they all have opinions.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.14.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they're all one make. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Manufacturers
The parts you've been fitting all along are Northstar Commercial's: sold everywhere, at list price, with few surprises. Now seven other makers want a word, and each has habits:
- **Ilyan Vector Works** (Ilos): engines with a fifth more thrust for a fifth less mass, sharper thrusters and a faster FTL drive. They run hot, cost more and wear 30-40% faster if you don't look after them.
- **Kesh Heavy Systems** (Keshar): engines, lander legs and drives that weigh half again as much, cost more, and wear at well under half the rate. Notoriously difficult to kill.
- **Gateworks Heavy Orbital** (Iron Gate): the big engine. 60% more thrust, twice the mass, and a heat problem you'll need radiators for.
- **Deep Junction Machine Cooperative** (Foundry): cheap engines, tanks and thrusters. Ugly, a bit weaker, and mechanics love them.
- **Orun Thermal Collective** (Orun): radiators that shed 40% more heat and last longer.
- **Aerial Massworks** (Ilos): radiators and tanks at a fraction of the weight. Their radiators wear fast if you skimp on repairs, which you will.
- **Roosevelt Modular** (New Houston): a bigger bolt-on tank, and a cheaper frame.

### Buying them
- Every yard sells every make. In the shipyard, each part now has its makers' versions listed under it. Hover for who makes it, what they claim, and how fast it wears.
- A maker's own system sells its parts **15% cheaper**. Everywhere else adds **10% import duty**. Starting at New Houston? Roosevelt's tank is a bargain. Vector Works engines are cheapest at Ilos, and so on. Shop accordingly.
- What you paid is what the part's worth on your books: remove it and that's what you get back.

### Elsewhere
- Wrecks now carry other makers' parts too. A Kesh Heavy engine off a derelict is a find; a Vector Works drive off one stranded far out is a find with a story.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Hull modules aren't branded yet: every corridor is the same corridor. Bigger holds and proper passenger cabins are next.
- Makers' versions look like their parts, in the maker's colours. Distinctive shapes are a job for an artist, which we don't have.
