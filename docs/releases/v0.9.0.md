Far Haul 0.9.0 for Windows, in which the captain stops doing everything personally, and starts paying people to do it instead.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.9.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and nobody in them wants a job. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load. If your ship already needed hands, they now have names, and opinions.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Hiring
- The **bar** now has people looking for a berth: four a day, different every day, from every people in known space, with names to match. Darak Venn will not answer to "Darak V."
- Each has a job, a skill from 1 to 5, a daily wage and something to say for themselves. Better hands cost more. They always do.
- Hiring costs a day's pay up front, and a free bunk. The starter has one bunk, and you're in it. Crew bunks are 3,000 cr at the shipyard; nobody signs on to sleep in the hold.
- **Letting someone go** costs two days' pay. Click once to think about it, twice to mean it. They don't come back the same day.
- Wages go on the running costs, every day, in port or out. The terminal's pay line names everyone you're paying.
- A big hold needs hands: one extra crew for every 40 SCU. A ship short of crew can't take freight until you hire.

### What they do for the money
- **Engineer:** everything wears slower, up to 40% slower for the best. Services worn parts in flight and between trips, and brings a failed one back if there's a spares kit aboard.
- **Deck tech:** goes outside so you don't have to. Fixes the hull's scorch marks while the engines are quiet, and on every trip: loose plating first, holes with your patch kits. Same limit as you: 50%, then it's yard work.
- **Pilot:** autopilot trips burn less fuel and take less time, between stars too. Get up from the helm on a run and the pilot takes the brake point. Go out on a space walk and they cut the engines first. They won't brake with you on the end of a tether; they'll tell you to come in.
- **Loadmaster:** lashes the load so knocks dent less of it. Unloads faster, and at the mining camp unloads it themselves, so the camp's crew doesn't take its cut.
- **Steward:** passengers pay more. A good steward's passengers say nice things about you, which is worth standing.
- The cockpit tells you what they've done. Trips tell you what they did on the way.

### Aboard
- Walk the ship and your crew are at their posts: the engineer in engineering, the pilot in the cockpit, the deck tech by the airlock, the loadmaster in the hold. Walk up to one for their name and their opinion.
- The HUD lists who's aboard.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Crew don't level up, get tired, fall out with each other, or ask for a raise. Yet.
- The loadmaster doesn't speed up unloading at the far end of an interstellar run; the consignee's dock sets the pace there.
- Crew stand at their posts. They don't wander the ship, and they're too polite to sit in your chair.
