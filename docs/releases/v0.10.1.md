Far Haul 0.10.1 for Windows, in which space gets busier, and not everyone who set out made it.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.10.1.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and nobody's stranded in them. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Not everyone is hauling freight
- **Station pods** shuttle between the station and the ships parked behind it: a cockpit, an engine and a lot of optimism. Small, slow and solid, so don't park on one.
- **Survey ships** come and go from the busier ports, legs folded out and jump drive aboard, off to look at something nobody's paid them to look at.
- **Ships in orbit**: ore tugs, tenders, patrols and relay techs circle the planet a few hundred metres up, and the last of them goes round its moon. You can fly into those too. Please don't.

### Stranded between systems
- On a run, now and then, there's a dead ship out along the route: somebody's jump drive gave up halfway and left them in the gap. The cockpit tells you when it's spotted, and it's tagged in orange, **DERELICT · FTL FAILED**, with its distance.
- Press **X** to brake while it's ahead and the autopilot stops you beside it rather than at your destination. Time compression eases off as you get close, so you don't blow past at x60.
- Its salvage panels come off with the torch like any other wreck's.
- Done with it? Brake off and on again and the autopilot carries on to where you were going.
- The same run keeps the same wreck in the same place, so it'll still be there when you've finished making up your mind.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Derelicts still only give up their hull panels. Opening them up for the cargo, supplies and parts inside is next.
- Pods, survey ships and orbiters don't answer hails. They have their own problems.
