Far Haul 0.9.5 for Windows, in which port control finally tells you what's wrong with your ship, instead of just looking at it disapprovingly.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.9.5.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Why won't it fly?
- "The ship isn't fit to fly this load yet" is gone. Now it names every check the ship fails, with the numbers: no FTL drive for a jump between stars, not enough thrust for the load (and what you've got), crew short, berths short, power, cooling, an open doorway, lander legs for a moon.
- The freight office says it up front, in red, above the buttons, before you press one that won't work. The ship remote says it too.
- A ship with no engine at all is told so, instead of being told its engines have failed. They can't fail if they were never there.
- For the record: an off-centre thrust line doesn't stop you leaving. It just makes the ship pull to one side, and costs manoeuvring fuel to fight it.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
