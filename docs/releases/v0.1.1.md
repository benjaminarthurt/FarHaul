Far Haul 0.1.1 for Windows: every system now looks like its own place, plus a pause menu and a round of polish.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.1.1.zip`, unzip it anywhere (a USB stick works) and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, so they travel with it, and saves from 0.1.0 still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Every system its own place
- Ports are built in the style of their builders: heavy Kesh steel, tall Ilyan halls, Vey water and haze, Orun stone arches. The multispecies hubs are a patchwork of everyone's fittings.
- Human colonies wear the livery of their work, each with a feature you remember it by: a crane rail, grow troughs, an ore track, sample cases, fish tanks, feed lines, container rooms, a departures board.
- Ports grow from cramped, worn frontier halls to grand core terminals with more people about.
- People are drawn from each system's species.
- Stations in space match: their colours, build and shape follow who built them and what the port is for.
- Fuel depots, belt works and moon stations are now their own kinds of site, inside and out.
- Moon habs and mining huts take their system's colours and style.
- Some bars and landmarks have names, such as The Long Layover at Port Meridian.

### New
- Pause menu on **F10** anywhere, or **Esc** in a port: resume, save, settings, main menu, quit.
- **F3** shows the frame rate.
- First-time hints in ports, habs and camps.
- Windows in the hab and the hut look out on the moon and your ship on its pad.
- A planet hangs beyond each station.
- The station's modules are solid to fly around.
- World sound: rooms, ports and the suit outside.

### Improved
- Moon ground, rocks and crystal finds look better, and people walk and look about.
- The cockpit view sits at seated eye height.
- Engine exhaust looks better.
- The builder's parts list scrolls.
- Long desk names fit their signs.
- The economy runs faster when time passes.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation.
- Prototype numbers: prices, pay and tuning will change.
- No space walks, planets with air or crew yet.
