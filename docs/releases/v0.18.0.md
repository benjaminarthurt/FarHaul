Far Haul 0.18.0 for Windows, in which space stops being empty and starts being inconvenient.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.18.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they're debris-free. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Debris fields
- Some routes have a debris field across them. The same field sits there for a month, then drifts off and another turns up somewhere else.
- Flying a run yourself, you'll see it ahead: a haze of rock with a marker, and a warning at the start of the run. Go round it, slow down, or let the autopilot thread it (V for course hold, X to stop at the target). The autopilot dodges what it can see and takes about a third of the hits.
- Every hit hurts more the faster you're going. At 2 km/s, a few hits is a bad day. At 100 m/s it's paint.
- Runs sent from the desk on autopilot roll the same field, at the autopilot's rate. Star jumps have fields on some lanes too. Passengers notice the bumps, and pay accordingly.

### Solar storms
- Every system gets the odd solar storm, a day or two long. The frontier gets more, because nobody out there was consulted about the stars.
- Some are forecast days ahead: on the radio, on the port's screens, and on the trade report. Some only show up on your own sensors a few hours out. Some just arrive. Out there it can always catch you.
- In port you're shielded. Out in one, the radiators lose up to half their cooling and wear fast, and the hull takes a little. Mind the heat.
- The other haulers sit storms out: none sets off to or from a system while one rages, so shelves dip, and the outposts feel it. Ask traffic control for clearance mid-storm and they'll suggest you sit it out too. Politely.

### Claim-jumpers
- Some salvage crews don't care whose beacon is on a wreck. They'll come to yours, cut faster than the honest ones, refuse to split, and ignore you when you tell them to clear off.
- You can report them. Sometimes a patrol is near and they run. Either way, once you're in port the port authority pays you a bounty on what they took, and your standing goes up. Revenge, with paperwork.

### Also
- The trade report's page 5 marks systems with a storm raging (red) or forecast (amber).
- A broken sentence on the info site, about station pods, now has a subject.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, at a dignified pace.
- Debris fields are only drawn on runs you fly. Star jumps meet theirs during the flash, which is a bad time to look out of the window anyway.
- Still no combat. The claim-jumpers are working on their nerve.
