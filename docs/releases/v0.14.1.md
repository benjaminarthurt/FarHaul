Far Haul 0.14.1 for Windows, in which the shipyard stops expecting you to memorise eight brochures.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.14.1.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they compare poorly. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Telling parts apart
0.14.0 gave you eight makers and a list of names and prices, which is how most people buy engines and how most engines end up in the wrong ship. Now:
- **Each make says how it differs.** Under its price, every maker's version lists what it does differently from the standard part: "+20% thrust · −20% mass · hot · wears fast", "+50% mass · tough", "−10% thrust · cheap". Brochure honesty, at last.
- **The comparison card.** Pick any part and the whole family lines up side by side: thrust (or cooling, fuel, lift, braking, push), mass, heat, power, wear rate, the price at this yard, and which system makes it cheapest. The best in each column is in green, the one you've picked in yellow.
- **What it does to your ship.** The card ends with what fitting one would change, before you pay: "T/W 0.19 → 0.39", "Heat 148/149 → 190/149 kW over!" in red when it would cook you, and fuel, lift, braking, jump speed or hold size where they move. Works for every part, including corridors, which mostly make you slower.
- **I** hides the card when you'd rather look at the ship.

### Known limits
- The projection assumes engines face aft and braking thrusters face the bow, which is where they go if you want to arrive.
- The card sits over the top of the view. On a small window it's I or squint.
