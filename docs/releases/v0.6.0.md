Far Haul 0.6.0 for Windows, in which the hull stops being a number you ignore.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.6.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they contain exactly zero hulls. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load, dents included.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Mind the station on the way out.

### It gets worse before it gets worse
- **Below 75% hull** the radiators crack and shed less heat. She runs hotter from here.
- **Below 50%** the tanks weep fuel in flight, up to about 1.5 t an hour by the end. You pay for every drop, including the ones space keeps.
- **Below 25%** every knock reaches the cargo, and the consignee docks the pay for whatever arrives broken.
- The HUD says which of these you've earned, next to the hull reading. So does the remote.

### Alarms
- Passing 75%, 50%, 25% and 10% sounds the alarm and puts a line in red where you can't miss it. Each mark is announced once. You'll remember.
- The ship remote shows the alarm as a red banner and buzzes the phone, so the sofa is no longer a defence.

### At 0%
- Nothing answers the helm: no thrust, no lift jets, no autopilot, no jump. You drift, with whatever spin you had.
- **Esc** (or **Call the rescue tug** on the remote) brings the rescue tug. It tows you to the nearer end of the run, like running dry, then the salvage crew bills about 8% of the ship's price, and about 30% of the load goes over the side or arrives in pieces.
- The hull stays at 0% until a yard fixes it.

### Port control has opinions
- Below 25% hull, no port will clear you to carry freight: not flown, not on autopilot, not jumped. Flying empty is allowed, at your own risk.
- At 0% the clamps stay shut. Nothing leaves, not even the helm for practice. You can still walk aboard and look sad.

### The repair desk
- Still does full repairs. Now also patches as far as your money goes, in 5% steps.
- Broke and below 30%? The yard will patch you up to 30% **on account**: enough to fly freight again, and you owe them for it. The bank will hear about this if you don't earn it back.

### You can see it
- Scorch marks spread over the plating as the hull goes. Cracked plates vent vapour below 50%. Torn wiring sparks below 25%. At 0% she does all of it at once, which is more than she does at full health.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Prototype numbers: leak rates, salvage fees and patch prices will change, usually after you've paid them.
- No space walks to patch the hull yourself yet. The yard thanks you for your continued business.
