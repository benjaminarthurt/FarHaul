Far Haul 0.13.0 for Windows, in which you finally go inside, and wish you'd brought a better torch.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.13.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and nobody's left a log in them. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Going aboard
- Every derelict has an airlock door, outlined in white with amber corners. Space walk over, take the **torch** (**2**) and hold **E** for ten seconds. Then hold **E** again to go in.
- Inside there's no power, no air and no gravity. Your boots hold you to the deck, your suit lamp goes where you look, and the emergency lights do their best, which isn't much.
- Your suit air keeps running the whole time. Run out inside and the tether winch drags you home, which is undignified.
- **E** picks things up. **L** at the helm reads the log. **E** in their airlock takes you back out. You can carry one thing at a time, and the trip home is long.

### What's in there
- **The log**: what went wrong, who took the pod, who didn't make it. A wreck stranded out on a route usually blames the jump drive. One near a station usually blames somebody.
- **The flight recorder**: in the cockpit. Hand it in at any port's freight office and the insurer pays a few thousand credits to find out what happened. More if it was a long way out.
- **Loose crates** in the holds and **lockers** of patch and spares kits, like the ones out of the cargo hatch.
- **The crew who didn't make it.** Carry them home. They take up a little hold space. At the next port's freight office, the port authority takes them back to their families and pays a recovery fee. Nobody makes a joke about it, and neither do we.
- **Now and then, someone who did.** Rare near the stations, less rare out on the routes. They get a free bunk if you have one, and the jump seat if you don't: every ship has one, for exactly this. Hand them over at a freight office for a 6,000 cr rescue bounty and some standing. After that they'll sign on with you, no hiring fee, or go their own way.
- **No bunk and the jump seat's taken?** Press **E** and report them. The port sends its own boat, and the freight office pays you a finder's fee (40% of the bounty) and a little standing. Nobody gets left behind for want of a bed.

### Port authority
- A new **PORT AUTHORITY** section at the freight office handles recorders, remains and survivors. The phone's Repairs tab does too.
- Survivors aboard count against your bunks, so the bar won't sign anyone into a berth that's already taken.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Derelicts are the same few layouts as everything else, so the floor plans will start to feel familiar.
- Survivors don't walk on their own. You clip them to your tether and lead them out.
