Far Haul 0.6.2 for Windows, in which the planet finally notices you're leaving.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.6.2.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they contain exactly zero planets. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Fixed
- **Jumping past a planet.** A world in view used to sit there through the whole spool-up, unimpressed by your faster-than-light drive. Now it starts drifting as the drive spools, slides off the edge of the screen as the ship accelerates, and is behind you a little over halfway into the streaks. The station goes the same way.
- **Arriving.** The new system's world comes out of the middle of the view as the streaks fade and slides out to where it belongs, so there's no longer a jump in the view when the approach starts.
- The arrow pointing at the station you just left no longer hangs around through the jump, pointing at something several light years behind you.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Everything in 0.6.0's notes still applies, including the salvage crew's invoices.
