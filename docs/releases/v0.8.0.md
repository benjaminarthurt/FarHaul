Far Haul 0.8.0 for Windows, in which the captain finally goes outside, on purpose, in space.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.8.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they contain exactly zero tethers. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Then check your air.

### Space walks
- Get up from the helm and go to the airlock. Away from a berth, **E** suits you up and puts you outside on a 30 m tether, clipped on by the hatch.
- Drift on the suit's jets: **WASD**, **Space** and **Ctrl** for up and down, **Shift** for more. Let go and the suit's stabiliser stops you drifting, eventually.
- The air is the time limit. There's a gauge for it, and it goes red when it's time to head back. Run out on the tether and the winch reels you in, gasping.
- **Q** at the hatch brings you back in. The pause menu can reel you in from anywhere, undignified but quick.

### Fixing the hull yourself
- The scorch marks on the hull are the jobs. Each one fixed takes about 6% of the damage off.
- Some are **loose plating**: take the **wrench** (**1**), hold **E** or the mouse button, and it's seated and bolted in four seconds. Free, apart from the dignity.
- Some are **holes**: take the **torch** (**2**) and a **patch kit**, and weld a plate over it. Patch kits are 400 cr at the fuel and repairs desk, eight to a locker.
- Wrong tool and you'll be told, in no uncertain terms.
- A space walk can't take the hull above **50%**. Past that it's yard work, and the yard would like you to know that.
- Welded patches stay on the hull until a yard repairs it properly.
- A breached hull can be fixed from outside too. One repair and the helm answers again, which beats paying the salvage crew.

### The tether
- Nobody's at the helm while you're out, but the ship carries on doing whatever it was doing. Coasting, it carries you with it and the tether hangs slack.
- Burning, braking or spinning, it doesn't. You're flung away from it and the tether takes the load. A strain gauge shows how hard it's pulling.
- Get flung down thirty metres of slack and it snaps. You're adrift. The jets still work, if the ship holds still long enough for you to catch it. Drift 150 m from the hatch, or run out of air, and a tug fishes you out of the dark and tows the ship in. It charges for that.
- So: engines off before you go out. You were warned, and the game says so again as you step out.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Only the hull can be fixed outside. Radiators, engines and thrusters wear out and break in a later version, which is something to look forward to.
- No space walks on moons. On a moon you walk; the suit's the same.
