Far Haul 0.9.2 for Windows, in which spare doorways stop being a design constraint and start being a line item.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.9.2.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they're full of holes of a different kind. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Door caps
- Two new parts in the shipyard, under **DOOR CAPS**: a plain **door cap** (250 cr) and a **door cap with a porthole** (600 cr).
- Bolt one over a doorway you don't need and the hull counts as sealed. No more building a corridor to nowhere just to shut a crew bunk's back door.
- Turn it to face the doorway. It won't fit any other way, which saves arguments.
- It's a plate on the doorway, not a box: the cell beside it stays empty to look at, though nothing else can go there while the cap's on. Take it off to build onward.
- The porthole cap has a square window at eye height. From inside you can watch space go by; from outside, the hull has a window where the door was.
- Light enough that the engines won't notice. Cheap enough that your accountant won't either.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- Caps are for doorways in walls. A ladder shaft's open top or bottom still needs a module over it.
