Far Haul 0.9.4 for Windows, in which you can change your mind about a load, and the planets stop being optional.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.9.4.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and they don't cancel anything. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Cancelling a contract
- Took a load you can't fly? Sold the jump drive with the freight still aboard? **Cancel this contract** at the freight office, the station terminal or the ship remote.
- Only before you set off, while you're still where you loaded it. Once you've arrived, deliver it.
- The load goes back on the dock and the shipper keeps 10% of the pay for the trouble (never more than you've got). Let down someone who asked in person and your standing takes a knock too.
- Press once to see the fee, again to mean it.
- The freight goes back on the board for somebody else, so the economy doesn't lose a crate.

### Solid planets
- The planet beside the station is solid now, and so is its small neighbour. Fly into one and you stop at the surface, bounce, and pay for the speed you hit at.
- Gently is fine. At orbital speed it's the whole hull, and the rescue tug's invoice.
- The cockpit tells you which world you hit, in case it wasn't obvious.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- The worlds in the sky are close-up scenery, not to scale. They're solid, not landable: moons are where you land.
- No cancelling halfway there. Space has no lay-bys.
