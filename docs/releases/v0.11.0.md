Far Haul 0.11.0 for Windows, in which the economy finally lets you see its books, and they're about as healthy as you'd expect.

**Download** `FarHaul-windows-0.11.0.zip` under Assets below. Not the Source code files: those are GitHub's, and their accounts are none of your business. Unzip it anywhere and run `FarHaul\FarHaul.exe`. Nothing is installed. Saves go in a `Far Haul saves` folder next to the exe, and saves from earlier versions still load.

**On first run** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the exe is not code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### The trade report
Press **N** anywhere in a game. It's also in the pause menu and at the freight office. The game waits while you read, which is more than the bank does. **1** to **4** or **Tab** switch pages, and **N** or **Esc** closes it.

- **Overview**: freight paid, goods delivered and tonnes moved across known space this week, against last week, with a month of history behind each. How many ships are working, how many jobs are waiting, and who's running out of what, worst first. Every system's prices against the going rate, so you can see where things are dear before you've loaded anything.
- **Flows**: a map of known space with the week's freight on its lanes, cargo streaming along them, and every ship between systems where it is right now, loaded or empty. Systems are ringed for whether they send out more than they take in. The busiest lanes are listed beside it, with what they mostly carry.
- **Companies**: every carrier company ranked by what it earned this month, with its fleet, how much of it is working, and its cash. You're in there too. Probably near the bottom. For now.
- **Your mark**: what you've hauled and earned, your share of all the freight in known space, your rank, the deliveries that reached a shelf that was running short, salvage sold, your takings day by day, and who you've supplied.

The world has been keeping notes. A new game opens with two weeks of history from the economy's warm-up. An older save starts its notes the day you load it.

### Known limits
- 64-bit Windows (x86_64). ARM Surface devices run it through Windows' built-in emulation, wheezing slightly.
- The report keeps the last 30 days. The economy has a long memory, but a short filing cabinet.
- In-system runs count towards your takings and share, but they don't draw on the known-space map. They're very small lines.
