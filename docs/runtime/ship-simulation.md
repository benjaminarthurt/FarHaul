# Ship Simulation Runtime

A Far Haul ship is a connected physical machine. Modules occupy space and connect through structure, electrical buses, coolant, atmosphere, water, waste, data and fuel networks. An intact component can still be unavailable when its supporting network is severed.

Acceleration derives from thrust and current mass. Cargo therefore changes performance. Placement contributes to center of mass, while reactors, drives and equipment create heat that must reach working radiators.

Damage is local and persistent. Temporary repairs can get a ship home but become part of its maintenance history. The intended result is closer to operating a commercial vehicle and small industrial plant than managing abstract RPG equipment slots.
