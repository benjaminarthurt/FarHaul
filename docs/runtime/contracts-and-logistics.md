# Contracts and Logistics Runtime

Jobs originate from simulated needs. A job board does not invent twenty tons of filters because the player opened a menu: a facility falls below its reorder point, procurement sources material, a supplier allocates cargo, and the unserved transport leg becomes a freight opportunity.

Rates have a cost floor and modifiers for urgency, handling, frontier exposure and service requirements. NPC carriers compete for the same work and consume real network capacity. Empty containers and specialist equipment must also move back toward demand.

Receiving inspection closes custody. Payment, damage claims and reputation consequences follow records generated by the actual shipment.
