# People and World Runtime

Persistent people have schedules, locations, relationships, obligations, memories and limited knowledge. A mechanic can remember years of reliable business; a broker can remember a disputed load; a rescued captain can carry an obligation forward.

Most population movement remains aggregate. Commutes, school trips, freight and leisure generate traffic flows; only nearby or consequential people need individual simulation.

World events modify existing systems. A storm closes a route, a festival raises passenger and hospitality demand, a utility failure consumes emergency stock, and a labor action reduces handling capacity. Events are therefore causes inside the simulation rather than random text popups.
