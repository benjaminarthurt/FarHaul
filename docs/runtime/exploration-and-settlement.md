# Exploration and Settlement Runtime

Unexplored systems are generated from stellar physics through disks, planets, atmospheres, geology, resources and hazards. The same deterministic seed always produces the same system.

Surveying creates information. Investment and jurisdictional processes can turn that information into expeditions and claims, but discovery alone grants neither sovereignty nor guaranteed profit.

Settlements progress only when recurring activity can support power, life support, housing, medical capability, governance and freight. They can stagnate or fail. Successful settlements create new persistent demand and eventually new trade routes, closing Far Haul's core loop: **commerce pushes exploration; exploration creates settlement; settlement creates commerce.**

Generated places also receive causal histories so an old town has infrastructure and scars consistent with how it grew.
