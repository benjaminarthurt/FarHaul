# Living Economy Runtime

Inventory is a ledger, not a mood. Every facility tracks on-hand, reserved, inbound, damaged, safety stock, reorder points, demand and lead time. Shortage labels are derived from days of cover.

Companies react to those ledgers. They procure, produce, price, hire, invest and charter transport using locally available information. Small firms can remain aggregate; companies that become important retain individual facilities, debts and contracts.

Prices are local and information-limited. A mine failure three systems away does not instantly change a shelf price unless traders learn about it or expected deliveries fail.
